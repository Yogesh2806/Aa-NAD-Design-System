Module Aa: “Aa” built from 8px-grid blocks with 4px corners, cut out of a rounded tile. The ink is NAD Ink `#111111`; the `-reversed` and `-white` files are `#FFFFFF` for dark backgrounds.

- `module-aa-nad-horizontal.svg`: primary logo for headers, docs and README.
- `module-aa-nad-stacked.svg`: square or centred spaces.
- `module-aa-nad-symbol.svg`: the tile on its own, for avatars and social.
- `module-aa-nad-symbol-small.svg`: 16–24px only; the blocks are merged so they read at favicon size.
- `module-aa-nad-blocks.svg` / `-blocks-white.svg`: the letters without the tile, for patterns and loaders.
- `module-aa-nad-wordmark.svg`: block “Aa” followed by NAD, without the tile.
- `module-aa-nad-app-icon-512.png`: app and PWA icon.

Clear space is ¼ of the tile height. The logo is never recoloured with a product's primary colour.
